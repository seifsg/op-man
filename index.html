How it counts

Push-ups

Phone flat on the floor under your face. The proximity sensor by the earpiece sees you come close: one rep.

Sit-ups

Phone on your chest or in your hands. The accelerometer tracks how far your torso tilts up and back.

Squats

Phone on your chest or held in front of you. The accelerometer feels your body drop and rise.

The plan

  • 10 → 100. All three start at 10 reps on day 1 and climb to 100 by day 100.
  • Voice and buzz. Every rep is spoken and felt, so you never look at the screen.
  • Streaks and history. Hit all three targets to keep the streak alive.
  • Private. No account, no cloud, no camera. Everything stays on your phone.
